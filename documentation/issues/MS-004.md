# MS-004 — Revision Capture: Vue viewers and engine lifecycle

Status **In review**. Owner **Coordinator**. Depends on MS-002 and MS-003. See the [architecture](../ARCHITECTURE.md), [design](../DESIGN.md), [capture tests](../../tests/capture.test.ts), and [retargeting tests](../../tests/retargeting.test.ts).

`MotionCaptureViewer` owns the capture and skeleton overlay. `Scene3DViewer` owns the character and orbit controls. MediaPipe and Three.js adapters are isolated, and engine initialization belongs to the viewers.

The migration retains video-only capture, the 30 Hz cap, late cancellation, background stop, original quaternions and axes, and a maximum device pixel ratio of 2. It cleans up listeners, the observer, the stream, and GPU resources. Existing characterization tests ran before the migration. The three Vue lifecycle tests were added during integration.

Browser acceptance covers the animated demo and orbit controls. Physical camera and mobile checks require separate hardware testing.

## Implementation

The adapters moved to `infrastructure/capture`, `infrastructure/retargeting`, and `infrastructure/three`. `MotionCaptureViewer` owns the video, overlay, and `ResizeObserver`. `Scene3DViewer` owns the renderer and its disposal. The session stops the stream. The composable cancels the animation frame loop and removes the `visibilitychange` and `pagehide` listeners. Constants, geometry, axes, slerp, and lerp from the POC were preserved. The geometry construction helper was reduced to four parameters with the same values.

## Verification

Four historical camera tests and five retargeting tests passed. Three Vue/jsdom tests cover mounting and a single disposal, WebGL failure, and observer removal. The demo, overlay, and procedural character were observed in desktop and mobile-sized browser viewports. Physical phone and performance checks remain pending.

Automatic approval review rejected the local browser action to enable the camera because explicit authorization to use this device's camera was missing for the session. Authorization was requested from the PO. Independent checks continued, and the physical camera acceptance remains open.
