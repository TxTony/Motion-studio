# MS-010 - Add Retargeting: progressive torso follow from shoulder rotation

Status **In review**. Owner **Coordinator**. Developer **animation_domain (gpt-6.2-sol, medium)**. Follow-up to [MS-009](MS-009.md). Contracts: [architecture](../ARCHITECTURE.md), [retargeting tests](../../tests/retargeting.test.ts).

## Authorized outcome

The PO approved the discussed heuristic: when shoulders turn relative to the pelvis beyond a tolerance, the torso progressively follows in both directions. MediaPipe continues detecting all landmarks. Body-part selection, independent head tracking, capture performance, camera settings, character geometry, and external-rig import implementation are out of scope.

## Data and behavior contract

Measure signed shoulder yaw in the existing pelvis frame, projecting the left-to-right shoulder axis into the plane perpendicular to the pelvis up axis. Use 3D world landmarks, not 2D apparent shoulder width. Positive yaw follows Three.js positive rotation around local +Y. Keep hips driven by the existing pelvis orientation. Drive spine and head world targets from pelvis orientation multiplied by the additional torso yaw; head follows the torso until independent head tracking is designed. Existing limb directions and conversion through parent world rotations remain valid.

Initial named response constants: 10 degree dead zone, gain 0.75 on the excess angle, maximum 45 degree additional torso yaw. Response is continuous at the dead-zone edge, symmetric and bounded; existing quaternion smoothing handles time continuity. These are provisional tuning values, not hardware-validated anatomy. A 30 degree shoulder/pelvis difference yields 15 degree torso follow; below 10 degrees yields zero. No automatic neutral-pose calibration or new control is introduced.

Require both shoulders at visibility >= 0.55 and finite, non-degenerate geometry for the extra yaw. Hold the last valid additional yaw when it cannot be measured, and clear it in reset(). Preserve the current whole-pose hold when the original pelvis frame is unavailable. Return toward zero on a valid neutral pose. Do not change PoseSample, RigPose, JSON, capture cadence, or recording contracts.

Human smoothness and latency are not established by synthetic angles. MediaPipe continues detecting all points; no body-part mode is added.
